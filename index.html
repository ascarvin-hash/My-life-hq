🧴 Product + Tool Guide

Glass / mirrors  Windex + glass microfiber
General hard surfaces  Mr. Clean Clean Freak + microfiber
Kitchen grease  Dawn Powerwash / kitchen degreaser + non-scratch sponge
Bathroom tub/shower  Scrubbing Bubbles + shower scrubber
Toilet bowl  Toilet bowl cleaner + toilet brush
Disinfecting touch points  Clorox wipes where surface-safe
Floors  Vacuum/sweep first, then appropriate mop + floor cleaner
Tight spaces  Detail brush / grout brush

📲 Add My Life HQ to Home Screen

iPad / iPhone

  1. Open your Netlify My Life HQ link in Safari.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Name it My Life HQ.
  5. Tap Add.

Android / Samsung / Pixel

  1. Open your My Life HQ link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Confirm.
Once installed, open it from the icon instead of from ChatGPT.
