Special Load Guide

Towels + Washcloths

Wash separately when practical. Avoid overloading. Dry completely before putting away.

Bedding

Check care label, avoid overfilling washer, dry fully, and put back on beds promptly.

Cleaning Cloths + Mop Heads

Keep separate from regular clothing when heavily soiled or covered in cleaner residue. Follow tool/material care instructions.

Delicates

Check label first. Use gentle cycle/mesh bag or hand wash if required. Air dry when appropriate.

Pet Bedding

Shake/remove loose hair first, wash separately when practical, and clean washer area afterward if needed.
